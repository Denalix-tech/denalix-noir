## Shared page structure

Each service page should use this information architecture:

1. Breadcrumbs.
2. Eyebrow and unique H1.
3. Concise outcome-focused introduction.
4. “Problems this service addresses.”
5. “What Denalix Tech can build or improve.”
6. “How the engagement works,” linking to `/how-it-works`.
7. Related services.
8. Consultation CTA linking to `/contact` or the existing contact anchor.

Avoid unsupported absolutes such as “best,” “guaranteed,” “instant,” or promises of a specific ROI/ranking. Avoid minimum word-count padding and repetitive keyword variants.
